# Original README artwork

Tool: built-in `image_gen` (original master drawing, retained for the README hero and how-it-works artwork).

```text
Use case: scientific-educational
Asset type: master earbud illustration for Pinch, a macOS app
Primary request: A single technically accurate Apple AirPods Pro earbud drawn as white technical wireframe line art on pure black (#000000). Three-quarter side view, silicone ear tip pointing upper left, rounded acoustic housing at top, short clearly visible straight stem descending toward lower right. Entire earbud fully in frame, centered with 15 percent clear margin. Outline and a sparse handful of curved construction contours describe its volume. Stem pressure sensor inset clearly defined. Uniform thin clean white strokes, black empty interior, crisp engineered drafting aesthetic. Sparse geometry, recognizable silhouette, suitable for reuse in a diagram and a tiny application icon. No dense mesh. Square 1024x1024.
Constraints: One earbud only. No text, letters, numbers, dots, annotation, leader lines, arrows, logos, shadows, shading, gradients, glow, colour, photorealism, case, hands or surrounding objects. All strokes even weight, approximately 5 pixels at 1024 resolution.
```

The generated original is `earbud-generated.png`. `render.py` normalizes its background to pure black and removes low-level raster noise for the README illustrations. Their SVG files embed the prepared drawing. All type, dots, arrows, leader lines, and diagram shapes are authored in SVG. No text was generated by the image model.


# Pinch icon redesign

The new icons are entirely hand-authored SVG, visually based on the original
AirPods Pro drawing. No new image generation was used.

Design brief:

```text
One AirPods Pro earbud, with two short curved pressure marks facing the stem,
one on either side. Leave a clear gap between each mark and the stem. White
line art on pure black, uniform stroke weight, rounded line ends. No colour,
gradients, glow, text, or hands. Preserve the original macOS rounded-square
path, 824 by 824 pixels, with 100px outer margins on the 1024px canvas.
For the menu bar, use black strokes on transparency and simplify the same
full earbud silhouette so it reads at 18 points.
```

- `icon-1024.svg`: editable, standalone vector source, with 24px white strokes.
  The silicone tip, housing, vent and short stem retain the original pose with
  fewer contours. Both pressure arcs share the same quadratic path; the right
  arc is an exact reflection (`scale(-1 1)`). The pair is rotated 15 degrees
  to match the stem axis. The black tile uses the original path verbatim;
  the area outside it stays transparent.
- `menubar.svg`: standalone 18-point template source, 1.25-point black strokes
  on transparency. It keeps the full bud and tip seam, omits the small vent and
  opening, and uses an exactly mirrored pair of pressure arcs. Both PNG sizes
  render directly from this vector source; the 36px image is the 2x version.
- `render-icons.py`: rebuild only the requested icons and review previews with
  `python3 assets/src/render-icons.py` (Python 3, Pillow and `rsvg-convert`).
  The existing `render.py` also invokes it after rendering the README artwork.
- `icon-preview-32.png` and `icon-preview-16.png`: actual-size downsampled app
  icons. `icon-preview-sheet.png` shows these and both menu bar PNGs at native
  size, above nearest-neighbor enlargements for pixel inspection.

Visual review: checked the full 1024px app icon, native 32px and 16px previews,
and menu bar template. Both pressure arcs remain separated and visible at
32px. At 16px fine bud detail softens, while the stem and pressure marks remain.
The menu bar uses the full bud; a stem-only fallback was unnecessary.
`hero.png` and `how-it-works.png` were not changed for this redesign.
